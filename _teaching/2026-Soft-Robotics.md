I had the opportunity to be Assistant Professor for the [Soft Robotics](https://onlineservices.polimi.it/schedaincarico/schedaincarico/controller/scheda_pubblica/SchedaPublic.do?&evn_default=evento&c_classe=889638&lang=IT&__pj0=0&__pj1=9e477a6c54e169fd45397a5f8bfe6937) course held by [Barbara Mazzolai](https://www.iit.it/it/people-profile/-/people/barbara-mazzolai).

The course consisted of a **project work**  where the students had to develop a soft gripper. Designing molds, casting soft appendages with different materials and comand them through tendon-actuation with basic electronics to achieve an objective chosen at the beginning of the course.

I was responsible to manage the lab session, aiding the students in developing their platforms. Guiding them through out-of-the-box solutions.
